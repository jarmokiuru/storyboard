        How it works

        Camera view: drag the picture to pan and tilt (it follows your finger); pinch or scroll to zoom the lens. Top view: drag the camera to move it, the tip of its yellow lines to aim it, the floor to look around; pinch or scroll to zoom. Tap the small view to swap. Add furniture and figures with Add; tap one to choose it, drag it in the top view to move it. To change several at once (e.g. every eye line), switch on Several and tap them, or use All figures; Shift-click on the Mac. A chosen figure shows dots: drag a hand or foot to move the limb, an elbow or knee to bend it, the blue dot to raise or lower the hips, the white dot to turn the head.
        Mac keys: 1–9 camera (board) of this scene, W A S D move, arrows pan and tilt, R / F up and down, hold Shift to go faster, Z / X roll, T top view ↔ camera view, [ wider lens, ] longer lens, Backspace delete the chosen piece, Esc let go of it (again: back to the board), Cmd-D duplicate, Cmd-B drop to the floor, Cmd-K Clay ↔ Outline, Cmd-Z undo, Cmd-Shift-Z redo.
