      Lens (mm)

      Camera view: drag the picture to pan and tilt (it follows your finger); pinch or scroll to zoom the lens. Top view: drag the camera to move it, the tip of its yellow lines to aim it, the floor to look around; pinch or scroll to zoom. Tap the small view to swap. Add furniture and figures with Add; tap one to choose it, drag it in the top view to move it.
      Mac keys: W A S D or arrows move, R / F up and down, hold Shift to go faster, Z / X roll, Esc back.
